## Day prompt

Use case: illustration. Asset type: high-resolution background painting for a full-browser living landscape website, wide landscape 16:9, ideally 2048x1152 or larger. Primary request: an exquisite quiet oil painting of a temperate English-style lakeside woodland cottage in late spring, a scene to contemplate for a long time. Composition: broad tranquil lake occupies the right half and lower right, pale sage-blue water with soft painterly horizontal reflections, curved shore going from lower middle to middle right. A small old honey-colored stone cottage with a weathered slate pitched roof and a chimney sits left of center around x=37%, y=55%; clearly visible doorway and two small dark windows. A winding pale earth footpath leads from the bottom center-left foreground to the cottage door. A large beautiful old deciduous tree with visible forked dark trunk frames the far left edge, canopy across upper left, a small branch reaching toward the cottage; tree roots and long grasses in foreground. Low layered misty hills along the far shore, horizon around 43% of image height. Delicate reeds, wild grasses, restrained cream wildflowers. Spacious pale blue-gray sky with a few broad pearly clouds, open upper right. Style/medium: authentic fine-art oil on linen, perceptible confident brushwork, subtle impasto, luminous broken color, atmospheric depth, painterly edges and lost edges, restrained natural detail, sophisticated tonal harmony. Quiet pastoral naturalism with impressionist handling. Palette: moss and sage greens, muted ochres, warm stone, silvery blue water, cream sky, no oversaturation. Lighting: soft diffuse warm afternoon light, gentle sky illumination, no strongly directional hard shadows, no sun disk. Mood: intimate, spacious, timeless, contemplative, beautiful even without action. Constraints: image fills edge to edge; no border, no frame, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no UI. No people or animals baked into painting, no flying birds, no smoke, no boat. The cottage and foreground tree must remain identifiable at wide view. Natural believable proportions, not fantasy, not cartoon, not photorealistic photograph. This will be the stable first art plate, with movement added separately in code.

## Dynamic sky, version 1

Prepared September 18–19, 2026. The original complete plates above remain untouched for fallback. New painted sources were created with the built-in image-generation tool, followed by deterministic native-resolution matting and export in `scripts/prepare-sky-assets.mjs`. No runtime image processing or new graphics framework is used.

| Export under `public/assets/lakeside-sky-v1/` | Dimensions | Convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sky-day.png`, `sky-night.png` | 1672 × 941 | Opaque, cloud-free, identically registered brush texture |
| `foreground-day.png`, `foreground-night.png` | 1672 × 941 | Straight RGBA PNG, byte-identical coverage alpha |
| `sky-mask.png` | 1672 × 941 | Grayscale; white is visible sky; foreground alpha = 255 − mask |
| `cloud-atlas.png` | 2048 × 1024 | Straight RGBA; neutral RGB brush shading; alpha is base density |

The daytime original is canonical geometry. The original night plate contributes regional illumination through an 8-pixel low-frequency RGB ratio, applied to canonical day detail. Original night window interiors are retained in small interior rectangles. This eliminates doubled day/night branches and avoids relying on the image model's approximate registration. It changes some night brush detail compared with the fallback; the original night image remains the fallback unmodified.

The oak uses an authored outer search envelope and native color matte, retaining transparent leaf gaps. The horizon uses a detailed traced contour with a narrow gradient-constrained continuous edge path. Partial edge colors are recovered from nearby fully covered foreground pixels within four native pixels. Coverage itself is not broadly blurred; partially covered silhouette pixels stay fixed in the renderer. Flat black, white, magenta, replacement skies, and five light blends are generated as inspection sheets. These are visibility masks, not depth maps for birds or other local effects.

Clear night sky uses the exact day brush registration with RGB scales 0.22, 0.31, and 0.43. There are no stationary clouds, moon, stars, or baked celestial glow in either sky plate. Cloud sources have transparent horizontal boundaries and are independently resized into two atlas interiors. Distant region: `(4, 4, 2040, 504)`; near region: `(4, 516, 2040, 504)`. Each interior has four duplicated gutter texels. An eight-texel transparent boundary guard makes the horizontal seam periodic and keeps vertical boundaries clear. RGB is neutralized; transparent texels carry neutral shading to avoid colored fringes.

All uploads explicitly use `UNPACK_PREMULTIPLY_ALPHA_WEBGL=true`, `UNPACK_FLIP_Y_WEBGL=false`, and no browser color conversion; pixel-store state is restored afterward. Shader samples are premultiplied and use explicit `over` once. A changed cloud alpha safely recovers straight shading before premultiplication by final density. Composition retains the original display/sRGB-value working space; linear-light color management is deferred.

The generated clear sky is retained as the runtime `sky-day.png`; the original transparent cloud source (1774 × 887) is retained at `artwork/lakeside-sky-v1/clouds-source.png`. With Sharp available, `node scripts/prepare-sky-assets.mjs` re-exports the bundle and contact sheets. Optional positional arguments select alternate clear-sky and cloud source files. Generation originals were also left in the tool's generated-images directory. The generated filenames were `exec-ad838ff2-410a-4e9a-bdb0-9da50fb6c3e2.png` and `exec-7a5864da-87f8-4c57-87a4-61e999ca25b0.png`.

Two independent horizontal cloud strips stacked, each within its own half of the image, with large transparent separation. Bottom half: more distinct rounded cumulus forms, varied density, subtly warm ivory tops and neutral medium-gray undersides, irregular painted edges and substantial gaps. Clouds must be neutral grayscale with maximum lightness .95, NOT blue sky. The brushwork must evoke a restrained English landscape oil painting, not photorealistic, not vector. Leave both left and right image boundaries fully transparent (no cloud touching edges); leave top and bottom of each half fully transparent; all clouds contained within bounds. No landscape, sun, moon, stars, text, border or checkerboard. Preserve partial transparency in thin clouds and soft edges.
